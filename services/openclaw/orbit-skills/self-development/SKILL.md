# Controlled Improvement

1. Analyze recorded outcomes and identify evidence-backed successful or unsuccessful patterns.
2. Produce a versioned proposal; do not change source code or production configuration.
3. Include evidence, confidence, affected business rule, risk, and rollback instructions.
4. Wait for a human approval recorded by Orbit CRM before any significant rule or knowledge change.
5. Never change access rights, financial terms, paid services, or confidentiality rules.
